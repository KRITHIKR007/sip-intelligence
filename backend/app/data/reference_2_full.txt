Kavach & Electronic Interlocking (EI) – 
SIP Symbol Register 
S.No. | Symbol / notation | Meaning and application 
Reference register for engineering review and AI drawing classification. The notation column is a searchable 
shorthand, NOT a replacement for the approved graphical symbol. Original IRISET SIP symbol plates are 
reproduced in the appendix. Kavach tag letters are from RDSO Annexure H, Amendment 5. Confirm project-
specific legends and the current approved standards before design use.

Reference notes 
 
The 2/3/4-aspect entries are classifications. Their aspect colours (R/Y/YY/G) must be checked against the 
approved aspect sequence chart and signal profile. 
 
IRISET page 23 states that the normally displayed aspect is drawn with a double line; graphical shapes in 
the appendix are the actual source symbols. 
 
Kavach Annexure H specifies letters N, S, T, X, D, G, L, A for tag placements. It does not establish a 
universal pictorial icon for every Kavach equipment item. 

